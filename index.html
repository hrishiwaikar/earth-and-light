Waikar Residence, Pune — Interior Design Master Plan

⬇ Download as PDF

For Mrs. Sadhana Waikar · 3BHK (converted 4BHK) · 26th floor (top), east balcony · July 2026 · Budget frame ₹20 lakh ±

Design language — “Earth & Light”: Japandi-inspired, earthy, Indian. A warm hand-finished lime shell into which wood and stone are placed like objects in a gallery. Colour arrives through material — teak amber, terracotta, ochre, olive, charcoal stone, unlacquered brass — never through gloss or ornament. Light is layered, warm (2700K, CRI≥90, dimmable), mostly indirect. Spacious, layered, never bare; never gaudy, never all-white, never themed.

The three signature moves (protect these in any value-engineering)

  1. Buddha water wall — living room north band: charcoal stone sheet-flow panel + seated basalt Buddha on a teak plinth, grazing light, planting flank. Vastu-north, visible from sofa, dining and entry. (Wet and dry variants — family decision W.)
  2. Attunements screen — 3-panel top-hung sliding teak screen (fine slats, linen backing) closing the healing room off the living hall. The single most important joinery item in the flat.
  3. Daylight mandir — altar on the mandir room's east wall, teak slats diffusing the east window: morning sun rises behind the deities. Tapered teak coffer overhead.

Porcelain deck tiles; heavy low planters only (hanging planters deleted); wind-tolerant species. Swing: slim bench at the south dead-end ONLY, slab-anchored after society NOC — else two cane easy chairs (decision B).

5 · Lighting (the biggest lever)

  • 2700K, CRI≥90, dimmable everywhere; 3000K only under kitchen cabinets. Written into the BOQ; reject non-compliant supply.
  • Cove-only false ceiling (100–150mm perimeter band, bare slab centre, 10–12mm shadow gap); AC bulkheads at edges; centre height ≥2.7m kept.
  • Three layers per room: cove ambient + task (pendants/reading/under-cabinet) + accent grazers (water wall, niche, mandir). Max 4 deep-baffle downlights in the whole living area.
  • Elder-eye rules: ambient capability ≥300 lux dimmable down; zero visible glare sources; motion night-path 20–50 lux bed→bath.
  • One trailing-edge dimmer family with a single driver compatibility matrix. Scenes: day / evening / session / pooja / night.

7 · Execution sequence

Civil/plumbing (water-wall drain!) → electrical chase + conduit → close & cure → ceiling coves → 1m² limewash sample & sign-off → wall finishes → joinery → stone veneer → water commissioning → lighting → soft furnishing.

8 · Ten written conditions for the design team

  1. No high-gloss laminate or acrylic anywhere.
  2. No PVC/WPC panels, printed-stone tile, or faux-wood flooring.
  3. 2700K CRI90+ dimmable in the electrical schedule; nothing above 3000K.
  4. 1m² limewash sample on the actual wall, signed off, before ordering (builder's acrylic putty may force a lime-effect plaster — decide by sample, not promise).
  5. All conduits chased and closed before any wall finish.
  6. The attunements room is never repurposed.
  7. Water-wall drain valve installed and demonstrated at handover (if wet option).
  8. One brass family for every handle in the flat.
  9. Swing only with society NOC + slab anchors; otherwise freestanding or chairs.
  10. Study joinery only after site measurement; all dimensions site-verified before orders.
